About SlugSpots

SlugSpots is a student-built platform to help UC Santa Cruz students find the best places to study on campus — and to help each other discover spots that don't show up anywhere else.

Why I built this

SlugSpots started as a personal side project, but it quickly became something I wanted to actually be useful for fellow slugs. I don't know every hidden study spot on campus — nobody does — so instead of trying to list them all myself, SlugSpots lets students suggest their own favorite spots, rate and review places they've studied, and see what other students recommend.

What you can do here

  • 📍 Browse an interactive map of study spots across campus
  • ➕ Suggest a spot you love (reviewed before it goes live)
  • ⭐ Leave ratings and reviews to help other students
  • 👍 Like your favorite spots
  • 🏅 Earn badges and climb the leaderboard for contributing

What's next

SlugSpots is still growing. More study spots, better photos, and new features are on the way — if you have ideas or spot something broken, suggestions are always welcome.

A heads up about load times

SlugSpots runs on a free hosting plan, which means the site can take up to a minute to load if it hasn't been visited in a while. Once it's warmed up, everything should run smoothly — thanks for your patience!

Your email is used only for account login and is never shared or used for marketing.
